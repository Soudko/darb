# Proposed places: sources (checked 2026-11-14)

Only official pages were used for facts. Positions come from OpenStreetMap (Nominatim search), rounded to 4 decimals.

## The Avenues (the-avenues)
- Position: https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/164140967 (29.3035, 47.9391)
- toilets = yes: https://www.the-avenues.com/kuwait/en/services
  Quote: "Accessible restroom facilities designed to provide comfort and convenience for guests with disabilities."
- seating = yes (existence only; accessibility not stated): https://the-avenues.com/kuwait/en/about
  Quote: "ample seating and gathering space for shows, markets, and performances" (Grand Plaza).
- Seen on the same services page but NOT mapped to a Darb fact (no matching key): wheelchair loan ("Wheelchairs and mobility scooters are available to help guests move around the mall comfortably."), sign-language support.
- Unknown: entrances, parking (the page says only 13,000-car parking and "Pre Booked Parking"; no accessible bays stated), lifts (not mentioned on the pages read).

## Al Kout Mall (al-kout-mall)
- Position: https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/311088084 (29.0801, 48.1418). A second OSM way (167494074) exists nearby.
- lifts = yes: https://www.tamdeen.com/16april2018.shtml (owner Tamdeen Group's own newsroom page, April 2018, may be out of date)
  Quote: "conveniently placed elevators and escalators" (car-park entrances).
- Seen but not mapped: "wheelchairs and mobility scooters for the elderly" among free customer services.
- Unknown: entrances, parking (multi-storey car park, no accessible bays stated), toilets, seating. The official mall site alkout.com returned no readable content.

## The Scientific Center of Kuwait (scientific-center-kuwait)
- Position: https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/314148911 (29.3496, 48.0895)
- entrances = yes (set by the lead after checking the page): https://tsck.org.kw/faq/ - Q "Is the venue wheelchair accessible?" A "Absolutely! Our facility is fully accessible"
  Quotes: "fully accessible" (no detail on step-free entrances, lifts or toilets); "ample parking on-site" (no accessible bays stated).

## Looked at and rejected
- 360 Mall: official site (360-kuwait.com) shows "under maintenance"; Tamdeen's news page has no facility facts.
- Sheikh Abdullah Al-Salem Cultural Centre: official site not reachable; only third-party pages found.
- Al Shaheed Park: official site (shaheedpark.da.gov.kw) has an expired certificate; not opened.
- Marina Mall, Grand Avenue, Souq Sharq (closed for renovation per press), Kuwait Towers: official sites not reachable or none found.
- Amricani Cultural Centre (Dar al-Athar al-Islamiyyah): official pages read list phone numbers only, no accessibility facts.
- Tareq Rajab Museum: official site lists hours and addresses only.
